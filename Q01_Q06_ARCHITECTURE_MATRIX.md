## Normal macOS routes and non-equivalent probes

`package_entry.main()` without args→verified bundled Core/WSP→`managed_onboarding.launch()`→`launch_bip39_trial()`→`Bip39FusionWindow`. Legacy option→`launch_managed()`→ManagedWalletWindow. Hidden `--ci-q06-bip39-spend`→`q06_spend_probe.run`; `--ci-q06-bip39-readonly`→q06_owner_probe; `--ci-q05-managed-wallet`→q05_packaged; `--ci-real-core-wallet-cocoa`→older wallet probe. These share primitives but do not share every user interaction/state transition.

Control path: Qt ControlWorker requests→WalletExecutor queue→one owner backend thread→original CoreSupervisor plus native wallet session. No GUI-to-GUI networking. WalletSession snapshot scans verified tip, complete history and reservation fingerprint. Prepare validates Intent, chooses eligible one-cluster coins, acquires draft and persists before returning review. Sign checks live tip/outpoints and native lease, persists signed before crypto. Broadcast checks exact review/signed PSBT, persists uncertain before RPC, validates exact returned txid, then clears transient review. Unknown result retains locks and faults. Only unsigned explicit cancellation releases drafts.

Q06 create/reopen seed is not spending activation. `_activate_q06` opens original anchored scanner, enforces irreversible reservations through sync, checks receive identity and returns verified snapshot. `_scan_q06` deliberately refuses to bypass an existing anchored checkpoint. `_reopen_q06` currently reconnects only seed metadata; this is the central Unlock transition gap. Independent mnemonic proof rescans the same original chain read-only and cannot recreate a different isolated chain's funds/history.

Native macOS/final app evidence does not establish Windows/Linux current source. Legacy wallet probes cover sign/broadcast uncertainty and reopen for Q05; Q06 probe's lock→open→activate bypasses normal unlock. Existing payjoin/disclosure/release/platform modules may be packaged or earlier work; their presence is not authority to begin unrelated Q tasks. Full conflicted/reorg lifecycle (Q12), multi-node (Q14) and full GUI/DPI (Q17) remain separate obligations.
